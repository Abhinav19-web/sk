About Us

Thoughtful design, honest execution

SK Interior Designer & Associates is a Mumbai based interior design studio in Kurla West. We handle every stage of a project, from the first space plan to the final finishing touches, so you deal with one team from start to handover.

Our work blends clean modern lines, warm lighting, quality materials and smart storage. Whether it is a compact flat or a full commercial fit out, we design for comfort, durability and your budget.

  • Residential and commercial
  • Custom cabinetry and millwork
  • Lighting and ceiling design
  • Single team, start to finish
